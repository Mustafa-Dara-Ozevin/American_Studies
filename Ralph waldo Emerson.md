

No one influenced American Poetry as much as Emerson. [[Walt Whitman]] and [[Emily Dickinson]] admired his poetry. [[Emily Dickinson]] mostly from his worldview and [[Walt Whitman]] from his worldview and technique. 

He also influenced [[Ezra Pound and Imagism||Ezra Pound]] and [[Allen Ginsberg and Howl |Allan Ginsberg]]

There are some similarities between Emerson and [[Edgar Alan Poe]]: Both lost their family members in young age and use their imagination to escape. But how they do it is different: While [[Ralph waldo Emerson]] leaned towards Spirituality, Poe leaned towards dark side of imagination. Poe Apollonian vs Emerson Dionysian 

- Has Puritan heritage, is a priest, well read on scripture.

- Poet sits between partial and perfect man
