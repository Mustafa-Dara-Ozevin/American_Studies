# The first wave of Korean immigrants 1903-1949  
  
## Summary  
First wave immigration mainly happened to Hawai. 
## Notes  

- 1884 first Korean to immigrate to America
- Most of them worked at pineaplle and sugar plantations in Hawaii 
- [[Chinese Exclusion Act 1882]] banned Chinese citizens from entering the United States. ==First act to discriminate basis of race==
- Chinese citizens being banned caused Korean to emerge as ===another source of cheap labour===.
- Missionaires establishing Korean Chruches to encourage migration to America
- Between 1905-24: 2000 Koreans moved t0 Hawavii and Califorina as **"Picture Brides"**
- [[The Oriental Exclusion Act 1924]] Bans all asian immigrants. The students was excluded from this ban
