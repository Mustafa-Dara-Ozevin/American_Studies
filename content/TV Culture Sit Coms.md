### TV & American Society

TV become ideological as the unifier of the fragmented postwar family. An emblem of "togetherness", of technology and advancement. It ==replaced the family icons of fire place and piano== around which people grouped around and socialized. 

![[Pasted image 20260413104438.png]]

Domestic living space, where family met and bonded, had become integrated with the space of the television. "A kind of household cement that promised to reassemble the splintered lives of families who had been separated during the War", "It keeps use together more" Claimed Atalanta Radio host.

==Reinforced== a precise a set of values and beliefs that are firmly rooted in traditional patterns of: family, Home, Gender, Class and Race. 

## [[Theodor Adorno]] => Culture Industry 



### Affirmative Culture

In late capitalist societies Individuals are already completely integrated into the society. So function of the affirmative culture is to reinforce that traditional beliefs


♯ Sit com as model as appropriate domesticity 

- Family is directly represented in the familiar environment isolated from outer world, focus is on the social relations rather than the external affairs
- Episodic, easily solvable problems 
- Rarely if ever included minority or marginalized characters
