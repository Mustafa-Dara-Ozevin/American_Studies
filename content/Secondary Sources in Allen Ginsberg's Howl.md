# Secondary Sources in Allen Ginsberg's Howl  
  
## Summary  
[[Allen Ginsberg]] American poet in Beat generation. Free, rebellious. Counter culture. 
## Notes  
- Jazz Aesthetics: Jazz music => repetition, improvisation, free-form, similar to bop jazz
- Spontaneous prose: stream of consciousness, long free lines. 
- Beat Generation: Literary movement in 50's. rejected traditional values, embraced experimentation and spiritual world
- Criticism of American Society: Capitalism, conformity, religion
- Moloch symbolizes industrial society, capitalism 
- Language and emotional power: Explicit expression, slang long flowing sentences
