Germany believed only way to win the war was a complete naval blockade of Britain with Submarines. They announced the seas around the Britain as war zone and warned that any ship travelling through it will be fired upon without warning. [[Woodrow Wilson]] claimed this was against international law and submarines had to warn each ship individually before the attack. German government protested sayings that surfacing to warn ships would leave the submarines entirely vulnerable to counter attacks and it was not an reasonable request.
