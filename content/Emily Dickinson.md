# Emily Dickinson  
  
## Summary
A poet from Boston, Massachusetts. Had a very isolated life, never married. Came from a wealthy family. Contemporary of [[Walt Whitman]]
## Notes  
- [[Emily Dickinson]] lived a isolated live while [[Walt Whitman]] had an urban, social life
- Famous for her botanic knowledge 


| [[Emily Dickinson]]                           | [[Walt Whitman]]                                |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Isolated, hermit-like, introvert life         | Urban, outgoing, social life                    |
| Art of arts sake                              | art of societies sake, socio-political concerns |
| Short poems                                   | usually writes very long poems                  |
| There is always a proper closure              | Open Ended                                      |
| Expresses her own sensibility and perspective | More universal perspective                      |
| Stanzaic verse                                | Prosaic                                         |
