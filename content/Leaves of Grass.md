# Leaves of Grass  
  
## Summary  
A foundational poetry collection by Walt Whitman, exploring themes of democracy, nature, and the individual self.  
## Notes  
- Showering with nature, going back to basics => *Simplicity*
- "I admire Myself" => Both cosmic and individual me
- Utilizes all 5 senses
- In his earlier days he tried to argue with people and tried to convince them. Now he witnesses and waits 
- "I believe in you my soul... The other I am must not abase itself to you" => 2 Equal sides: Soul and intelligence 
- Childlike innocence vs Adulthood. Whitman identifies himself as the child 
- Grass => Simplicity, common hood that can flourish anywhere. Just like american Democracy
- Grass = "Beautiful uncut hair of graves" => Acknowledge America's bloody past
