# performance theory  
  
## Summary  
Erving Goofman's dramaturgical approach is the most famous impression management theory in sociology. 
## Notes  
- Individual are metaphorical performers managing the impressions they present to others.
- Individuals curate the performance to convey their desired image to their audience(Society)
