# Social Network  
  
## Summary  
Directed by David Fincher, Screenplay by Aaron Sorkin. Based on "The Accidental Billionaires" by Ben Mezrich. Got Best adapted screenplay, original score and film editing 

> Who put the elf with no friends in charge of human communication - ERB



![[Pasted image 20260518104741.png]]

## Notes  

### Main Conflicts
- Friendship vs ambition
- Innovation vs ethics
- Social connection vs emotional isolation
- Power vs responsibility

### Main Characters

- Mark Zuckerberg
- Eduaordo Saverin
- Sean Parker
- Winklewoss Twins
- Other characters are underdeveloped because we see the movie through Mark's perspective and he does not care enough about other characters to get to know them

### Narrative Technique
- Flashbacks
- parallel editing
- deposition scenes
- rapid dialogue 

### It is About
- The birth of platform capitalism
- Digital identity
- Online self-performance
- Surveillance culture
- Loneliness in the age of connectivity


### The film demonstrate how social media allows people to:
- Curate identity
- Perform status
- Seek validation
- Gain visibility

### Facebook becomes:
- A symbolic system of popularity
- A digital hierarchy

### Relates to
- Erving Goffman's [[performance theory]]
- Digital Self-fashioning
- Online Persona construction

### Film presents
- Elite male competition
- misogyny
- emotional immaturity
- status obsession

### Critics note the film explores
- Traditional masculinity
- Competition rooted in pride
- gendered power structures

### Important concepts 

- Platform capitalism
- Data commodification
- digital labor
- network economy

### Sean Parker introduces

- Silicon Valley Ideology
- Disruption culture
- wealth obsession

### Despite millions of online connections

- Characters become lonelier
- relationships collapse
- communication weakens 
### Key Question

Can Social media truly create social connection?
