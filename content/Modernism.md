# Modernism

as an international art covers many avant-grande styles like surrealism, futurism, Surrealism, Impressionism(only for writing). **Modernism is international**. Fore bearers were Darwin, Nietzsche and Marx, it's intellectual guru was Freud.

## Historical background

emergence of *New Women*, the peak and downturn of British Empire, unprecedented *Technological change*, downturn of British empire, the rise of *Labour party*, the appearance of factory-line *mass production*. War in Africa, Europe and elsewhere. Literature of not just change but **crisis**.


# Modernity

First used by *Baudelaire* in the mid 19th century:
- Industrialization
- Urbanization
- Secularization
certain new understanding of *space* and *time*: Disintegration and reformation, Fragmentation and rapid change. **Chaos** and **cultural revolution**.

Differently theorized by Emile Durkheim, Max Weber, Ferdinand Tönnies

Durkheim: Division of labour
Weber: Disenchanment of a rationalized world
Tönnies: From Close-knit rural community, The **Gemeinschaft**, to the heterodoxy and anonymity of urban society **Gessellschaft**
