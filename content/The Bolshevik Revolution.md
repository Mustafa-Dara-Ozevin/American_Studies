In 1917, the liberal-democratic government of Aleksander Kerensky was overthrown by radical socialists led by V.I. Lenin. Lenin seized power vowing to change world politics and end imperial rivalries in a way that challenged [[Woodrow Wilson]]s. Lenin desired a global revolution carried out by workers that would sweep away the "Imperialist order". The idea of Bolshevik style revolutions spreading was frightening to Western leaders which caused the [[Red Scare]].
