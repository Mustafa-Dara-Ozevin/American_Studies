### The Kellog-Briand Pact (Pact of Paris)

- Aimed to simply abolish war
- "we can outlaw war system just as we outlawed slavery and the saloon"
- signed in 1928 multilateral agreement attempting to eliminate war as an instrument of national policy
- Limits on naval armaments
- pledged 62 nations to "Condemn recourse to war for the solution of international controversies, and renounce it as an instrument of national policy"
- Was considered just a moral statement because it lacked enforcement.
