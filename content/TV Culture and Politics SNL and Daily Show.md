## Satire

Brian A. Connery and Kirk Combe state in their work Theorizing Satire, " The one thing we know about satire is that it promises to tell us what we do not want to know - what we may - in fact resist knowing"

> Satire is a literary Trojan Horse for which polite ( or puublic) artfulness produces and dissemblng form, serving first to contain and conceal, and then to unleash primary concept of the satirist 


## Sarcasm 

- Sarcasm's main intention is to "bite" and to hurt the target thus catching and the attention of the public making them laugh through their tears. 
- Comes from sarkasein in Greek meaning "to tear fresh"

## Functions of Satire

> Ruben Quintero: Satirist is assumed to have our best interest at their heart and trying to steer us to a better reality 

> Satirist is the watchdog. No one should expects a watchdog to do a double duty of warning others of the barn fire and putting out at the same time 


### Political Satire

- Kind of satire that intends to expose and criticize foolishness, corruption or hypocrisy in politics.
- Anthony Thai: Satire has made politics more accessible, leading to more informed viewers
- There is no single media institution that embodies every element of the cultural, technological, political and aesthetic evolution embedded in the history of television. However, ==Saturday Night Live(SNL)== comes as close as any program does
- SNL was born in 1974 in the hearth of a classic network era, a time during which Powerhouses NBC, CBS and ABC fought to win a bigger piece of a pie which is essentially a three-piece pie
- SNL has consistently and intentionally debated the politics, pop culture and social norms of American llife in five different decades 
- SNL used political satire, especially impersonation of political figures and American presidents.
