Tensions between Germany and US escalated when British intelligence intercepted and published a telegram message from German minister of Foreign affairs Zimmerman to German minister in Mexico telling that "If Mexico joined a military alliance against U, Germany would help them take back the territories lost in 1848". Zimmerman hoped to "Set new enemies on America's neck, enemies which give them plenty to take care over there". [[Woodrow Wilson]] saw the telegram as "conspiracy against the well being against this country" and soon after asked Congress for "armed neutrality". He requested authority to arm american merchant ships with any armament necessary for self preservation. Anti-war senator saw the bill as a blank check to drag the country into the war and fillbustered it to death. Wilson armed the ships anyway
