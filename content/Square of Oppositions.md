# Square of Oppositions  
  
## Summary  
- The categorizations of [[Categorical Proposition]] leads to emergence of some natural interference about truth value of each other
## Notes  


![[Pasted image 20260522112733.png]]

- There are 4 relations between different categorical claims:
	- ==Contradictory==: between A <=> O and E <=> I. Have *opposite truth values*
	- ==Contrary==: Between A <=> E. *Both can't be true*. All other combinations are possible. 
	- ==Sub-contrary==: Between I <=> O. *Both can't be false*. All other combinations are possible. 
	- ==Sub-Alternate==: Between A => I and E => O. One way relation. If Universal is true particular is *also true*
