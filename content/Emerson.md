Nature and Soul

Soul = me
Nature = everything that is not me 

Emersonian trinity composed of Man, Nature and God(Oversoul) => Organic unity

Man can reach God, can reach nature, or can reach God through Nature

Art is the continuity of Nature. Human operations are so insignificant they do not vary the result.

Art is related to God

Art and Nature comes from same spirit, Their essence is one. 


### Beauty 

The standart of beauty is the entire circuit of Natural Forms -- The totality of Nature. Nothing is quite beautiful alone, nothing but is beautiful in the whole. Art is the nature passed through alembic of man. 

### Spirit

The world proceeds from the same spirit as the body of the man. it is a remoter and inferior incarnation of God. Disunity between spirit and body. Distancing from Nature means distancing from God

non-conformism
being a individual in the crowd
