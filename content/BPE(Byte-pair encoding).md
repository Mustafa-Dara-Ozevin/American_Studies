# BPE(Byte-pair encoding)  
  
## Summary  
[[BPE(Byte-pair encoding)]] is one of the most widely used [[Tokenization]] algorithms. It works by merging most common character pairs in the words and expanding its [[Word type]] corpus 
## Notes  

- **BPE training**: The BPE training algorithm iteratively merges frequent neighboring tokens to create longer and longer tokens. The algorithm begins with a vocabulary that is just the set of all individual characters. It then examines the training corpus, and finds the two characters ==that are most frequently adjacent==.
