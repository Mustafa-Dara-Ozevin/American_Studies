## [[Ferdinand De Saussure]] & Semiotics

Radically broke with 19th century linguistics and philology. Language, is a system of signs which should not be studied diachronically, but synchronically. *Structuralism*: What is important is structure of the language not history of it. 
- Signifier: Sound-image or its graphic equivalent denotation 
- signified: Concept or meaning
Relation between signifier and signified is **arbitrary** and **conventional**. *Cultural* and *historical* convention also governs the relation between the whole sign and what it refers to.


## [[Jean Baudrillard]]

Applies [[Ferdinand De Saussure]] semiotics to media literacy. Prominent essay **"The Gulf war didn't take place"**:

> There is no interrogation into the event itself or its reality; or into the fraudulence of this war, the programmed and always delayed illusion of battle. or into machinisation of this war and its amplification by information, not or mention improbable orgy of material 


### Simulacra

Modern society lost the belief in connection between its *symbols* and *real*. It counterbalanced the order by producing symbols that **did not refer to any reality directly**. Which [[Jean Baudrillard]] calls the simulation.
