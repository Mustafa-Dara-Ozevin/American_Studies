# Western Palace  
  
## Summary  
Story of [[Moon Orchid]]([[M. Hong Kingston]]'s aunt) coming to America and facing of with her husband who married again and started a new life in America. Takes place during the [[Vietnam War 1955-1975|Vietnam War]]
## Notes  
- [[Brave Orchid]] waits for her sister [[Moon Orchid]] in Airport for 4 hours because they calculated the arrival time wrong. [[Brave Orchid]] scorns at her children for always wandering around and asking for food.

| Moon Orchid                 | Brave Orchid |
| --------------------------- | ------------ |
| Thin                        | Fat          |
| Wears her jewelry out       | Modest       |
| Passive                     | Active       |
| Pragmatic                   | Pampered     |
| Voicing                     | Silent       |
| Worked in China and America | Never Worked |
|                             |              |

- [[Brave Orchid]] sees [[Fa Mu Lan]] cutout as just a "baby doll" while [[M. Hong Kingston]] saw her as a inspirational hero
- [[Brave Orchid]] is restless to talk about how they would confront [[Moon Orchid]]'s husband. [[Moon Orchid]] delays the conversation, saying she needs to rest first
- [[Brave Orchid]] pushes her to confront her husband and his new wife. [[Moon Orchid]]'s is reluctant saying she doesn't want to bother him.
- [[Brave Orchid]] advises [[Moon Orchid]] to make life unbearable for the second wife so she leaves to make a second home.
- We learn that [[Moon Orchid]]'s husband only send money never letters
- [[Moon Orchid]] can't voice his objections during the confrontation with her husband. She can only cry and whimper while his husband scolds her for coming to America uninvited.
- [[Moon Orchid]] grows hysteric, [[Brave Orchid]] tries to perform a chant to bring her to senses but it doesn't work.


> "The difference between mad people and sane people is that sane people have variety when they talk-story. Mad people have only one story they they talk over and over"

- She is admitted to mental asylum at last. She feels happy and understood in the Asylum. 

> We Speak the same language, the very same. They understand me, and I understand them
