# Cuban Missile Crisis  
  
## Summary  
*Briefly summarize the concept here.*  
## Notes  

- Embraced by failure in Bay of Pigs [[Kennedy]] vowed to bring down Castro.
- CIA had many failed assassination attempts
- Soviets decided to secretly deploy nuclear missiles to the island in 1962. They hoped the presence of such weapons would *deter any attacks* and would improve SU's standing in a nuclear confrontation 
- Kruschev accepted removing the missiles from The Cuba if US pledged to *never attack Cuba* and *withdraw the nuclear Jupiter missiles from Turkey*
- Cuban Missile crisis was the turning point in soviet-american relationships. Steps were taken to *cool the tensions*. 
- A coded wire-telegraph "**Hot line**" between capitals allowing near instant communication
