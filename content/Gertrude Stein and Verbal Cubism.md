## The continous present

She believed literature should act like movie instead of telling a story about the past. She using -ing suffix a lot which aimed to prevent the reader from thinging what happened before or what will happen net 


Semantic Dislocation(Verbal cubism)

Just like cubist Stein Broke her sentences into pieces. And showed it from many different angles by utilizing repetition and fragmentation 
. Rearrange the sentence till it loses traditional meaning. Turn it into a visual art piece 


"A rose is a rose/ is a rose is/ a rose"

By repetetion she strips the rose from its romantic meaning and turns into a physical object and a pure sound.
Sound is the most important aspects of her poetry
