# Brain trust  
  
## Summary  
The Brain Trust was a group of close advisers to Franklin D. Roosevelt, many of whom were academics, who played a crucial role in shaping the policies of the New Deal. They provided the intellectual framework for the legislative activity intended to combat the Great Depression.  
## Notes  
* Key members included Columbia University professors Raymond Moley, Rexford Tugwell, and Adolf Berle.
* The group helped draft various New Deal programs and presidential speeches.
* They favored government regulation and intervention in the economy to restore stability.
* Their influence marked a shift toward using expert knowledge and social science in federal policy-making.
