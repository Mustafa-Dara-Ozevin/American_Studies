# Allen Ginsberg  
  
## Summary  
Born to Jewish family in 1926. Was a prominent figure in many different social and literary issues. Known for being the founder of [[Beat Generation]]
## Notes

### Howl
- manifesto of [[Beat Generation]]:
	- Sick of militarism, consumerism, conformism, mainstream [[American Dream]]
	- Dedicated to Carl Solomon
- Style & Form:
	- Stream of consciousness
	- Broken into lines in which he would needs to take breaths
	- Anaphora => inspired by [[Walt Whitman]]
	- cataloging
- Tone:
	- angry and critical of capitalism, materialism, industrialism, the establishment and sexual represent
	- Vulgar language caused a lot of fuss. Trial following it created the Beat movement

### Kaddish
- is about heartbreaking biography of his mother who spent most of her adult life in state of mental torment
- his relationship with his mother played a important part in shaping his future ideology 
- Technically it is very similar with his other works
- tone is mostly about forgiveness and letting unburried memories go

### Social Matters

- Vietnam war, gay rights, drugs
- Accredited with coining the term *"Flower Power"*
