# F.D Roosevelt  
  
## Summary  
Franklin D. Roosevelt (FDR) was the 32nd President of the United States, serving from 1933 until his death in 1945. He led the country through the Great Depression with his New Deal programs and directed the nation's efforts during World War II.  
## Notes  
* He is the only U.S. president to have been elected to four terms in office.
* FDR implemented the "Fireside Chats," using radio to communicate directly with the American public and restore confidence.
* His leadership saw the massive expansion of the federal government's role in the economy and social welfare.
* He suffered from polio, which left him paralyzed from the waist down, a condition he largely kept from public view.
