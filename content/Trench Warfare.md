# Trench Warfare  
  
## Summary  
Trench warfare was a type of combat characterized by troops fighting from extensive systems of fortified ditches, most famously used on the Western Front during World War I. It resulted in a brutal stalemate and immense casualties for both sides.  
## Notes  
* Life in the trenches was miserable, with soldiers facing mud, rats, disease, and the constant threat of artillery fire.
* The area between opposing trenches, known as "No Man's Land," was often filled with barbed wire and craters.
* New technologies like poison gas, tanks, and improved machine guns were developed to try and break the deadlock of trench warfare.
* The psychological impact on soldiers was profound, leading to conditions like "shell shock."
