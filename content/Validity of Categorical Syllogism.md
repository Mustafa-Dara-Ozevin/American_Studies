# Validity of Categorical Syllogism  
  
## Summary  
The premises or the content of the syllogism is not related to **validity** of it. Argument made from false propositions can still be **valid**.
## Notes  

| Things      | Relevant Properties |
| ----------- | ------------------- |
| Terms       | Distribution        |
| Proposition | Truth Value         |
| Argument    | Validity            |

### Distribution
- a *term* in a [[Categorical Proposition]] is distributed iff(if and only if) the [[Categorical Proposition]] tells us something about ==every member of the term== in question
- The *Subject term (S)* of the proposition is *distributed* iff the proposition is **universal** 
- The *Predicate Term (P)* of the proposition is *distributed* iff the proposition is *negative* 


## Rules of Validity
1. The *Middle term (M)* should be *distributed* ==at least in one of the propositions==.
2. if a *term* is __distributed__ in the *conclusion* it should be __distributed__ in the *premise*.
3. *Premises* can not be **both** be *negative*.
4. If one **premise** is *negative* **conclusion** should be *negative*. if **conclusion** is *negative* one **premise** should be *negative*.



## Types of fallacies 

### Rule 1
- fallacy of the undistributed middle 

### Rule 2
- Illicit minor Fallacy
- Illicit Major Fallacy


## Ethymeme

A *syllogism* with one of the propositions missing
