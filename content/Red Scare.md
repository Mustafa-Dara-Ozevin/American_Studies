The suppression of dissent that often targeted German Americans during the war shifted focus to organized labor post war. When Soviet leadership announced formation of Communist International whose purpose was to export revolution around the world in 1919, fears grew in America. Labor union leaders was determined to retain wartime bargaining rights and secure better wages to compensate rising prices. Instead of negotiating Employers responded with rescinding the recognition of unions. Result was rash labor strikes and red scare
