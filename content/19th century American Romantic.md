## Notes  

### [[Edgar Alan Poe]] 

- Born in Richmond, North Carolina 
- Had actor parents, dead left the family when he was young. Mather died in stage coughing blood
- Had a very poor childhood, adopted by Allan's family 
- Has 2 surnames Allan(adopted), Poe(Original)
- All woman in his life died
	- His mother
	- His foster mother
	- his aunt
	- His school teacher
	- His childhood bride
- Turning death into object of desire 
- Only gained fame after his death in America but raised interest in Europe by Symbolic Poets during his lifetime
- Rumored to have opium and alcohol addiction
- Had a multiple jobs(editor, bookstore worker)
- Moved constantly: Richmond → New York → Philadelphia 
- Father of Dark Romanticism: [[Charles Brockdem Brown]], [[Washington Irving]] earlier examples but gained fame with Poe. 
- European Gothic vs Dark Romanticism: Dark Romanticism focused on dark side of the mind. While European Gothic focused generally on Transgressive religious monk 
- Also the originator of Detective genre
- Todorov Fantastic riding the line between supernatural and regular explanation 
- Supernatural always also has a regular explanation to go with it although it may be unlikely
- [[The Philosophy Of Composition]]: Solving a mathematical poem
	- Poet should decide on a specific effect and direct everything in the work to the achievement of that effect 
	- **Refrain**: Chorus, repeating single word which should include sonorous letters. 
	- Keep the poem short, excessive length dilutes the effect
	- Beauty is the sole legitimate province of the poem: Poetic beauty → Harmony. Death of a beautiful woman is most poetical topic in the word.
	- Language must be suited to the material in the Poem 
	- Just as the language must be natural to the material, so mus the rhythm 


### To Helen

- Represent the idealization of all the woman Poe lost in his life.
- Roots of Western civilization in antiquity 
- Yearning for the past that was unsoiled by modernity
- Purity of the woman
- Turning death into an object of desire
- Sickly pleasure
- Early western civ. ≡ childhood of Poe
- Helen's beauty remind of the old times, Trojan war.
* Aligning himself with all those men who died in the name of Helen
* Begins with physical desire, ends with spiritual desire(Psyche)
* Holy Land could refer to Athens, Jerusalem or Richmond
* Sonorous sound of sorrow

### Eldorado[^1]

- Eldorado stand for higher hopes and dreams. Once purpose in life
- Capitalism and American Dream are Eldorado of America
- Also can mean wisdom
- Eldorado as refrain
- Pessimistic message 

### Anabel Lee

- Gothic setting → shore, old castle. Natural forces can destroy the castle and childhood very easily. 
- Gothic characterization: Childhood lovers, Anabell Lee died very young. Narrator sleeping in her grave, also Gothic characterization.
- Haunting of the past, return of the repressed
- Refrain → Anabel Lee
- Emphasis on childhood 
- Separated because of Class difference(highborn kingsmen)
- Separation disturbs preserved state of childhood
- Gothic reunion → spritually together
