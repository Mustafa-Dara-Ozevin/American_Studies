# Disjunctive Syllogism  
  
## Summary  
Unlike a [[Categorical Syllogism]] where every propostion is a [[Categorical Proposition]]. Only one Propostion of of [[Disjunctive Syllogism]] is a [[Disjunctive Propositions]].
## Notes  
| A or B | Categorical proposition | Conclusion | Valid |
| ------ | ----------------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| A or B | A                       | Not B      | ❌     |
| A or B | B                       | Not A      | ❌     |
| A or B | Not A                   | B          | ✅     |
| A or B | Not B                   | A          | ✅     |
- Two 2 uses of "or":
	- ==Exclusive Or==: *Only one* of the *Disjunct* can be true. Truth of one excludes the other.
	- ==Incluse Or==: At least 1 Disjunct is true and *possibly both*
- ==Exclusive Or== is used in special cases that have contracidtory clauses. However if Logic we are only dealing with letters. Any logical connection between them, if exists, is unkown to us.
- Thus we ==always assume Inclusive Or== in logic
- Symbolizing => Turning standard english to [[Sembolik_mantık_MOC|Symbolic Logic]]
- ===Or == ∨===
- ===Not == ~===
