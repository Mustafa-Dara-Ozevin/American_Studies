After [[The Bolshevik Revolution]] in an attempt to embrass the capitalist governments bolsheviks published secret deal make between allied nations to detailing how to divide central power territories in case of Allied victory. To control the reputational damage this caused [[Woodrow Wilson]] unveiled a 14 points, reaffirming America's commitment to an international system governed by laws. first 5 point called for:
- diplomacy in the public view
- Freedom of the seas
- lower tariffs
- reductions of armaments
- decolonization of empires

the next 8 points specified the evacuation of foreign troops from Russia, Belguim and France, and appealed for self-determination for nationalities in Europe such as poles. The 14th point was, according to Wilson, the mechanism to achieve the others: a [[League of Nations]].
