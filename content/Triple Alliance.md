# Triple Alliance  
  
## Summary  
The Triple Alliance was a secret agreement between Germany, Austria-Hungary, and Italy formed in 1882. It was one of the two major alliance systems in Europe that contributed to the outbreak of World War I.  
## Notes  
* The alliance was primarily a defensive pact, with each member promising to support the others if they were attacked by two or more great powers.
* Italy eventually remained neutral at the start of WWI and later joined the opposing side (the Allies) in 1915.
* The alliance was intended to isolate France and maintain the balance of power in Europe.
* Along with the Triple Entente, it created a situation where a localized conflict could quickly escalate into a general European war.
