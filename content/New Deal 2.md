# New Deal 2  
  
## Summary  
The Second New Deal (1935-1938) represented a shift in FDR's policies toward more aggressive social and economic reforms. This phase focused on long-term security and the protection of workers and the elderly.  
## Notes  
* The Works Progress Administration (WPA) was established to provide millions of jobs through public works projects.
* Major legislative achievements included the Social Security Act and the Wagner Act.
* This period saw a greater emphasis on labor rights and social safety nets compared to the first phase.
* It faced significant opposition from conservative groups and the Supreme Court.
