# League of Nations  
  
## Summary  
The League of Nations was an international organization founded in 1920 after World War I to provide a forum for resolving international disputes and maintaining world peace. It was a key component of Woodrow Wilson's 14 Points.  
## Notes  
* Despite Wilson's advocacy, the United States Senate refused to ratify the Treaty of Versailles, and the U.S. never joined the League.
* The League's primary goal was to prevent future conflicts through collective security and disarmament.
* It had some success in the 1920s but ultimately failed to prevent the aggression of Axis powers in the 1930s.
* The organization was replaced by the United Nations after World War II.
