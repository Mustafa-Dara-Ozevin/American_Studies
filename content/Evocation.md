# Evocation  
  
## Summary  
Evocation is like a taboo. You need to create an image in the readers mind without using the words direclty related to it.
## Notes  
1. Show, Don't tell
2. Filtering
3. Active Verbs
4. Cast judgement in all directions not in none
5. Rythmn should not dstrupt the narration
6. Don't give a bulletpoint description give details that matter
7. Move the reader through: Words => images => Experience
8. Appeal to 5 senses:
	1. Sight
	2. Sound 
	3. smell
	4. taste
	5. touch
9. Significant detail != lots of detail
10. Concrete + meaningul => Siginificant detail
11. Pathetic syndrome
### Detail and Character
- a list of pyshical charactericstic does not neccesarily create an individual character
- avoid describing characters through generic categories alone 
- Instead, use details that reveal how the oersın behaves
- ask:
	- What does the character do?
	- What does the character choose?
	- what does the character notice?
	- what does the charater speak?
	- what objects surround the characters?
### Showing Emotion: The imaginery of Emotion
- Simply naming the emotion is usually weak:
	- she was afraid
	- she was scared
	- etc
- ==Emotion is experienced physically==
- Therefore, writers can respresent emotion through:
	- bodily sensations
	- gestures
	- actions
	- changes in perception
	- reaction to surroundings
- emotion becomes more convincing when it is dramatized rather labeled
- emotions are often fixed rather than pure
- a character may experience several contradictory feeling simultaneously
- "If I cannot use the emotion directly, what would the characters body actually do?"
