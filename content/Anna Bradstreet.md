# Anna Bradstreet  
  
## Summary  
Was one of prominent [[American Poetry Colonial Period]]. Come from a succesfull family. His father was the founder of the colony she lived in. Famous for her satiric criticism of Patricarchial Puritan Society
## Notes  

***The Author to Her Book***: 
- Talks about anxiety of publishing and impossibility of perfection
- Child is a metaphor for her poems
- Understatements about her poetry, ironic slighlty comedic overtone
- Heroic Couplet
- Iambic Pentimeter

***In Memory of My Dear Grandchild Elizabeth Bradstreet***:
- Written for her granddaughter who died at 1.5 years of age
- Stated she is happy for her because she is in heaven
- Says baby going to heaven much earlier than expected is a sign of God


***To My Dear and Loving Husband***:
- Unusual topic for Puritan society
- Marriage to Husband = Marriage to God
- Use of Overstatement/Hyperbole
- 11th line with preserverence gives away to Puritan background
