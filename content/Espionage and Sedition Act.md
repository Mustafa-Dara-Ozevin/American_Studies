The [[Woodrow Wilson]] administration compelled the obliging congress to pass "Espionage Act (1917)" and "Sedition Act (1918)".

 - **Espionage Act:** forbade "false statements" designed to impede the draft or promote military insubordination. It also banned materials that are considered treasonous from mails.
 - **Sedition Act:** Made it unlawful to obstruct the sale of war bonds and to use "disloyal, profane or abusive" language to describe the government, the constitution, the flag or the military uniform.
