# Plot And Structure
  
## Summary  
Plot is organization of events into a meaningful sequence.

## Notes  

1. ***Exposition*** => What does the reader know before the main conflict begins
2. ***Inciting incident*** => Which event sets the main story in motion
3. ***Rising Action*** => Which event male the situation increasingly difficult?
4. ***Climax*** => What is the decisive turning point
5. ***Falling Action*** => What happens after the decisive point
6. ***Resolution*** => What is the difference at the end?

- It connects events through cause and effect
- A plot makes readers ask *not only "What happens next?"* but also *"Why does it happen"*
- Event should contribute to [[Plot And Structure]] or [[Characterization pt1 || character]]


### Story vs Plot

| Story                                      | Plot                                               |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Events arranged in the chronological order | Events connected through causality and signifiance |
| Emphasizes what happened                   | Emphasizes why it happened                         |
### Conflict: The Foundation of Plot

- ==Conflict is essential==
- it can occur between characters or within a character
- Conflict creates tension and keeps readers interested
- A protagonist needs a ==meaningful desire or goal==
- obstacles, opposition and risks make that desire dramatically significant
- *Without conflict*, a sequence of events *feel uneventful*

===Desire + Danger = Drama===

- compelling protagonist wants something intensely
- The desired outcome must matter to the character
- Achieving the goal should involve risk, difficulty, or uncertainty
- Danger can be physical, emotional, psychological, or social 
- The reader becomes invested by anticipating whether the character will succeed

### The arc of the Story
- A story moves from a initial situation toward a significant change
- Conflict develops through a series of complications.
- Tension increases as the protagonist faces different obstacles
