- He was a poet, painter and most famously an Engraver.
- became blind in later periods of his life.
- Had an opium addiction.
- **Exhibitionist**.  
- Had hallucinations starting from his early life
- Famous for 2 poem collections: Songs of innocence, Songs of Experience



### Sick Rose

![[Pasted image 20260312135104.png]]


At first sight looks like a simple poem which is about a rose that is sick.

biblical allusion to garden of eden, Fall from Heaven, 

Rose represents: Ignorance

Main idea is **transformation** 

Criticism of State/Patriarchy

*historical interpretation*: War of Roses. Establishment of Tudor Family
