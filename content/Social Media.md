# Social Media  
  
## Summary  
Social media is a general name for forms of electronic communication through which users create online communities to share information, ideas, personal content and other content 
## Notes  

- 1997: *Six Degrees* => **First** Social Network
- 2000s: Rise of *Myspace*, *Facebook*, *Youtube*
- 2010-today: 
	- Mobile First platforms
	- influencer culture
	- *Tiktok* boom
- 2018 [[Barrack Obama]] presidential campaign effectively used Social Media
### American Cultural Values & Social Values

- **Individualism**: Personalized profiles and content
- **Freedom of Speech**: Debates over ==censorship vs Free Speech==
- **Consumerism**: Targeted advertising + influencer marketing
- **Celebrity Culture**: Social media creates ==micro-celebrities== and ==localized fame==

### Psychological and Social Impacts

| Positive Effects             | Negative effects                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Community Building, activism | Anxiety, Depression, [[FOMO]]                        |
| Instant communication        | "Compare and Despair" culture                        |
| Identity exploration         | Social media addiction and shortened attention spans |

### Social Media & American Politics

* **Polarization**: Filter bubbles and echo chambers intensify idealogical divides.
* **Activism**: \#BlackLivesMatter, \#Metoo, March for our lives mobilized millions
* **Disinformation**: Spread of fake news, deepfakes, and conspiracy theories  

### Social Media and American Youth

- **Shaping identity**: memes, Tiktok trends, and Viral Culture influence self-image.
- **Language Change**: Abbreviations (lol,brb); emojis and memes as a ==communication form==.
- **Mental Health and Social Validation**: Likes as a form validation and social status 

### Memes as Cultural Commentary

- Now a dominant form of *expression* and *resistance* especially between the youth
