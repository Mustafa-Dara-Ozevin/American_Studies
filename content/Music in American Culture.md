### 1920's Jazz

Pianist Jelly Roll Morton described New Orleans as the "cradle of Jazz". The early development of the genre in the New Orleans was the continuation of community life in New Orleans.
- Rich cultural heritage rooted in the African American experience. Since its inception in the early 20th century, Jazz has contributed to and been a reflection of American Culture. İt is widely considered to be the only truly original American art form.
- Occasionally Jazz compositions speak directly to political and social issues of the day such as "strange fruit" by Billie Holiday, "We Insist"
- Pioneering Jazz cats:
	- Louis Armstrong: Trumpeter, bandleader, singer, soloist, film star and comedian
	- Duke Ellington
	- Miles Davis

### 1940's 1950's - Rock'n'Roll
in the 1940's, Afro-american expressions of gospel, and blues had a baby and called it a rock and roll. That in turn gave birth to a happy, bouncing infant called rock. which grew up to have multiple offspring by different partners as psychedelic, punk, metal.
- Newer generations loved Rock and roll.
- Older generation and churches called it satanic
- Because of its origins in the lower class it was considered tasteless for upper classes 

### Soul

A genre born of the Afro-american experience, soul music sprang from the blues clubs, churches and street corners of the US. where R&B, gospel and doowop rang out in Chorus When those three styles collided Blues was founded. **Ray Charles**, **Anne Franklin**

A greater importance was placed on the on appearance of the musicians and gimmicks. Micheal Jackson dominated the 90's with his pure talent and dancing.
