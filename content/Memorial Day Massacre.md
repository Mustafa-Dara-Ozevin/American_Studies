# Memorial Day Massacre  
  
## Summary  
The Memorial Day Massacre was a violent incident in Chicago on May 30, 1937, where city police shot and killed ten unarmed demonstrators during a steel strike. The event became a rallying cry for the labor movement and highlighted the intense conflict between workers and corporations.  
## Notes  
* The demonstrators were supporters of the Steel Workers Organizing Committee (SWOC) who were striking against Republic Steel.
* Police fired into the crowd of men, women, and children as they marched toward the Republic Steel plant.
* Newsreel footage of the event was initially suppressed but later revealed the extent of police brutality.
* The massacre occurred during a period of increased labor militancy and the rise of the CIO.
