# Second Hundred Days  
  
## Summary  
The Second Hundred Days refers to the summer of 1935, when the Roosevelt administration pushed through another massive wave of reform legislation. This period solidified the legacy of the Second New Deal and dramatically expanded the American social safety net.  
## Notes  
* Often called the "Second New Deal" launch, it included the passage of the Social Security Act.
* The Wagner Act (National Labor Relations Act) was a key component, protecting the right to unionize.
* The Revenue Act of 1935 ("Wealth Tax Act") was also passed during this time.
* It reflected FDR's move toward more progressive and redistributive economic policies.
