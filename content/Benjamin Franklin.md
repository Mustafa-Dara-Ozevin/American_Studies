# Benjamin Franklin  
  
## Summary  
Statesman, poet, essayist. 
## Notes  

- in 1722 in his seventh [[Dogood paper]] he ridiculed standart elegiac verse that was popular in [[American Poetry Colonial Period]] by providing a DIY "Receipt to make a New England Funreal ELEGY":
	- TAke one of your neighboors that died lately: Better if it was a sudden death
	- Toss in some "Virtues, exellences" more if corpse is female
