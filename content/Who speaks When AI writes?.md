# Artificial intelligence as functional trickster in cultural production

Henriata Krupa


# Opening Question
- What happens when texts have no author?

- AI is not just a technology
- AI functions as a **digital trickster**

- Performs structural operation analogues to trickster operation logic


## Trickster Archetype
* Ambiguity 
* Deception
* Shape-shifting
* Inversion
* Bricolage
* Boundary-crossing
* Function: **Cultural Transformation** through *Transgression*


Trickster operates at the limits at the system changing it from the within

- *AI as Ambiguous And Anomalous* => Semantic coherence without aim
- *AI as deceiver* => Deceiving not consciously but structurally
- *Shape-shifting* => 
	- Stylistic Fluidity
	- Adapts styles 
- Situation Inversion =>
	- Monopoly to shared access
	- Author-text hierarchy disturbed 
- *Bricolage* => 
	- Recombines fragments
	- creativity as recombination
- *Boundary-crossing* => Collapses multiple boundaries: 
	- human/machine 
	- Author/text 
	- original/copy
	- authentic/simulated
