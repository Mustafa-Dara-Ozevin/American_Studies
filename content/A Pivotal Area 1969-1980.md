# A Pivotal Area 1969-1980    
## Summary  

## Questions
1. How did American Foreign policy change as a result of involvement in [[Vietnam War]]?
2. Why did Americans see this era as an age of limits?
3. Some historians describe the period between 1968 and 1980 as a time when many Americans lost faith -- in their government, in the possibility of consensus instead of conflict. Do you agree, or were the struggles and divisions of this era similar to those of previous decades
## Notes  
- Also called a *"Me decade"*
- Oppressive Domestic Policy
- Divisions deepened, movements for social justice and racial equality become stronger and more radical
- Highlighted the *distinctiveness of black culture*, believed *integration would mean subordination* 
- ==Black is Beautiful== motto for the movement

### Colorful Activism

Mexican Americans => Brown Power
African Americans => Black Power
Native Americans => Red Power

Native American activism:
- in 1969, a small group called "Indians for all Tribes" occupied Alcatraz Island demanding land to be returned to Indian peoples.


### The Women's Movement (2nd Wave)
The rights gained:
- Married woman to obtain credit in her own name
- unmarried woman to obtain birth control
- to serve in juries
- end of sex-segregated help wanted ads

- Opposition to Woman's movement
- STOP-ERA movement

### Gay Liberation
- Period of Discrimination 
- Consensual sexual intercourse was illegal in almost every state
- Until 1973 homosexuality was labeled a mental disorder 
- Symbolic beginning of the movement: Police raid on a gay bar in Greenwich village(1969) For the first time police was protested in **Stonewall Riots**
- **Gay Power**
- Affirmative action => [[LB Johnson]] believed that just having equal rights were not enough there also should be equal opportunities:
	- "Not just legal equality... but equality as a fact and equality as a result"


### [[Nixon]], [[Kissinger]] and the World
- [[Nixon Doctrine]]:
	- Us would continue to provide financial aid to allies in Asia and elsewhere but they should not count on US troops
- [[Detente]]:
	- Measured cooperation with Soviets. Comes from the French word for "relaxation"
	- The major aim was the check soviet expansion and limit the soviet arms buildup. 
	- Control the revolutions in the third world

### Crisis of Leadership & [[Watergate Scandal]] 
- Interlocking political scandals of the administration of U.S. Pres. Richard M. Nixon that were revealed following the arrest of give burglars at DNC headquarters in the Watergate office-apartment-hotel complex in Washington D.C..
- Facing likely impeachment for his role in covering up the scandal. Nixon became the only U.S. president to resign.
- The [[Watergate Scandal]] shook the confidence of American netizens in the their government. 
- I also prompted Congress to reevaluate the balance of power between executive and legislative branches:
	- War Powers Act
	- Budget and Impoundment control act
