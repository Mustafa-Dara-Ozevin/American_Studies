# Triple Entrente  
  
## Summary  
The Triple Entente was an informal understanding between France, Great Britain, and Russia formed in 1907. It served as a powerful counterweight to the Triple Alliance and was a key factor in the geopolitical landscape leading to World War I.  
## Notes  
* It was built upon a series of bilateral agreements, including the Franco-Russian Alliance and the Entente Cordiale between Britain and France.
* Unlike the Triple Alliance, it was not a formal military pact but rather a commitment to cooperation and mutual support.
* The Entente was motivated by mutual concerns over the growing power and ambition of Germany.
* When WWI began, the members of the Triple Entente (along with Italy and the U.S. later on) became known as the Allied Powers.
