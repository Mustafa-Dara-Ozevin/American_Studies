# Sylvia Plath's life and famous Works  
  
## Summary  
Sylvia Plath remain one of the most influential today 
## Notes  
 - Born on October 27, 1932 Boston
 - Her mother Aurelia Schoenberg Plath
 - Father Otto Plath: Died when she was 8 years old and became a mythical figure in her life
 - Graduated Smith University with high honors
 - She committed suicide by carbon monoxide poisoning in 1963

### The literary Evolution
Early writing: high technical skills, traditional forms, strong rhyme control
Last period: Fleeing imaginary
Perfectionism both strength and burden
Cultural Expectations 
Confessional Poetry
### Daddy

- Confessional and personal voice
- Violent and extreme images
- Love and hate coexisting
- Patriarchy and authority
### Lady Lazarus
- Theatrical tone and performance
- Biblical allusion
- Death and rebirth
- Dark irony and defiance

Changes the gender of Lazarus in biblical myths transforming it to a story about woman survival, suffering and struggle 
### The Bell Jar

- Confessional and hones tone
- Mental illness and depression
- identity and entrapment
- Female oppression and patriarchy
