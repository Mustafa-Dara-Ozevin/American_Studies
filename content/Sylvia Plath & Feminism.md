# Sylvia Plath & Feminism  
  
## Summary  
Feminism => is the belief that woman should be allowed to have some rights, power and opportunities as man
## Notes  

### Plath as a feminist figure
 - Plath's poetic voice challenges domination, expresses female rage
 - represents survival in man's world
 - Daddy => The loss of a father & women oppressed by men
 - Lady Lazarus => no matter how hard patriarchal society tries to humiliate women they will rise again 

### Plath as not a feminist figure
- individual trauma, artistic concerns etc that is not explicitly political 
- Plath wanted to excel as a perfect wife which is against feminist ideals
