# Walt Whitman  
![[Pasted image 20260514130934.png]]
  
## Summary  
American poet influential for his collection "Leaves of Grass", pioneered free verse, and associated with transcendentalism.  
## Notes  
- Laying on the grass = Interconnectedness
- Universal idea of man
- Talks from the pov of cosmic singularity 
- All differences are dissolved into the Cosmic self hood => Heterogeneous Homogeneity
- Union of opposites
