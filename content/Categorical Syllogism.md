# Categorical Syllogism  
  
## Summary  

Categorical syllogism is a proposition with 2 parameters. 

## Notes  


1. All Bulldogs are dogs
2. All dogs are mammals
3. All Bulldogs are mammals 

A valid categorical syllogism

S - minor term of the premise
P - Major term of the premise
M - Middle term of the premise 

Formal structure of a [[Categorical Syllogism]] starts with *Major premise* followed by *Minor Premise* and *conclusion*. *Content* and *Form* can be separated.

S => Bulldogs
P => mammals
M => Dogs

All M are P
All S are M
All S are P
AAA


It can be further condensed with Mood and figure of the form
Mood is the 3 letters signifying the letter of the Categorical proposition shown on the table at [[Categorical Proposition]]. Figure is a number signifying the order of the terms.

for Mood => AII we can infer:

All ..... are ...... (M and P)
Some ...... are ....... (S and M)
___ 
Some S are P 
But we can  not know the ordering of M/P and S/M that is when the Figure comes in.


Figure is a number between 1 and 4 that tells us the ordering of the terms
