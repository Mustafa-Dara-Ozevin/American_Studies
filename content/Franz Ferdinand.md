title: "Franz Ferdinand"
tags:

  - american-history
---

Heir to the Austro-Hungarian empire who was assassinated by a Serbian nationalist in June 28, 1914 while on a state visit to the capital of Bosnia. Austria-Hungary consulted their ally Germany on the situation and was advised to take a hard line. Serbia called on its Slavic ally Russia for help which in turn looked at France for help.
