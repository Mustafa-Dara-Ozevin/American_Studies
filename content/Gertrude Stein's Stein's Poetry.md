Challenged traditional meaning through repetetion, fragmentation and uncenventional language 


She was a inventor who treated words as an objects with autonomous, self referential meaning 


Her home was the crucible of invention for the **Lost Generation** 

Section 1 Repetition

She layered meaning onto a word with several repetitions. Each repetition added additional meaning and challenged the idea of fixed meaning

Section 2 Fragmentation 

She refused linear continuous narrative progression. Sentences break off, images juxtapose without clear connection, and syntax seems deliberately disconnected. Forcing the reader to actively connect the ideas themselves  


Section 3: Language as object 


treats language as an independent material object rather than a transparent communication medium.

Breaking it up to parts in a way that mirrors Cubism
