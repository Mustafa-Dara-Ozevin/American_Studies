A poem from [[Lyrical Ballads]] written by [[William Wordsworth]]. 

- has a simple grammatical structure and message
- Solititude of the romantic poet
- There is a divide at the 19th line of the poem. First 18 lines are a descriptiive pastoral poetry while after 19th it becomes introspective. 
- Emotion gathered in tranquility after the daily life is over. 
- Recollection of past expreinces and extraction of feeling from them.
- Language plain enough even an innocent child might be the source of them.
