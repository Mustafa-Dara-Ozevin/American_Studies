Collection of Poems by [[William Wordsworth]] and [[Samuel Taylor Coleridge]] although Coleridge's contribution was miminal. 

Wordsworth initially didn't want to write a preface and thought it to be a foolish endevour. But was convinced by his close friend to write one in the fears that his poems were too different to be understood without a preface.

He believed writing a preface like this would but too many restrictions on the author and could highlight some parts and understate some parts when they are equally valuable.

Preface of the Lyrical Ballads was written by Wordsworth to justify and define their new style writing: **"Romanticism"**. In this movement *intuition* not the *reason* was hailed as the champion. 

Features of Romantic poetry:
- **Freed from** any metric **restriction**
- incidents and situations **from common life**.
- Language used by real man and the same time, throw over them a certain colouring of **imagination**
- Often subject was **Humble** and **Rustic**: Because their feeling are not spoilled by civilizations yet. **Innocent feeling**. They speak plainner language, their elementary feelings is easier to understand and **more durable** 
- Simple and uneloberated expressions

**Purpose of Romantic Poetry**: "*Spoentenous overflow of powerful feelings: it takes its origin from emotion gathered in tranquility*"

Who is a Romantic Poet:

- [x] more lively sensibility
