The belief that it was America's mission to lead the world into an era of unobstructed commerce, free-market capitalism, democratic politics and open diplomacy. American Progressivism had to projected into the entire world.

"America had the infinite privilege of fulfilling it's destiny and saving the World" claimed [[Woodrow Wilson]]. He aimed to dismantle empires to honor the principle of self determination. And he believed all of this was only possible if Britain leaves the great war victorious.
