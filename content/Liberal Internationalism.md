# Liberal Internationalism  
  
## Summary  
Developed after [[World War 2]] slight alternative to [[Realist thinking]]. Starts with same axioms but more optimistic. [[Kant]] is one of the founding fathers
## Notes  
- Belief in Progress
- Capitalism is the best way to organize a society
- In favor of Non Govermental Instutions
- Interdependence can prevent wars
- Democratic Peace theory
- Virtous Cycle instead [[Realist thinking]] Vicious Cycle
