# Brief story summary
AI takeover of military supercomputer named AM after the WWIII, merging different countries' AMs
- Allied Mastercomputer, Adaptive Manipulator, Aggressive Menace
- Name AM: I think therefore I am 
- Humanity is extinct except for 5 people. Benny is the narrator.
- Video game of the same name, 1995

# Form & narrative
- Employed punch code tapes that spells "Cogito ergo sum" as chapter breaks 
- AM's hate towards humanity is described as form of of computer text through the circuit metaphor
- FORM not an external shell but a strong reinforcer of artificiality and AI consciousness
- Narrator mistakenly thinks his mind in not touched by AM

# Themes
- Misuse of technology
- War, especially cold war
	- AM is a war machine given consciousness: "It has been planned as creator of war"
- religious symbolism
	- Chanty cult like tone coupled with biblical references
	- AM is depicted as the God of the religion
	- "Deranged daddy" that likes to torture people
- Human condition, the ultimate question in speculative fiction
	- Foretold AI reducing humans cognitive capabilities<
