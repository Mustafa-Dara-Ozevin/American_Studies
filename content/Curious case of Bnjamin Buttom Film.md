Symbolic aging: Born old becomes younger: unnatural flow of time 
Aging doesn't define maturity 
Separation between physical age and emotional growth 

Key point: His body moves backward his mind and experience goes forward



Moving backwards symbolizes:
- Our desire to go back in time 
- Regret and longing for the past 
But shows that:
- Even if time reversed, loss and change still happen
Message:
Time cannot be truly controlled even if its reversed 


Isolation and Difference

always out of sync 


Everyone feels out of time at some point of their life 


Love and Timing

Benjamin and Daisy's relationship only aligns briefly 

They meet in the middle when both are physically similar age

Symbolism

Love depends on timing
Life doesn't wait for perfect moments

Message:
Even perfect love is limited by time 



Childhood and innocence 

Benjamin ends life as a child 
This reverse the normal idea of: growing wiser with age 

Symbolism:
life ends with innocence expect with experience 


The inevitability of Time

Despite the reversal:
- People still die 
- Relationships still end
- Time still moves forward for the world


Conclusion


Reverse aging is a metaphor not just a plot device 


Life can only be understood backward but it must be lived forward
