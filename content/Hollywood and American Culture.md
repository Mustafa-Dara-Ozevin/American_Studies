### Hays Code

in 1948, the Supreme court ruled that movie studios couldn't own Movie theaters that showed only their films. This was the beginning of the end of the Golden Age of Hollywood. big Five forced to sell the theaters and be more selective about the films they produced

Movie studios were also bound by the Hays Code, a voluntary set of rules of censorship in movies. While not a big issue in 50's it tied their hands when their audiences grew more liberal in 60's

### Hollywood 10

in 1947 The House unamerican Activities Comittee (HUAC) decided to investigate communism in films. 40 people in the industry were called to testify.

10 directors and screenwriters known as Hollywood 10 chose to protest was eventually jailed.


### Second Golden Age of Hollywood

1960 and 70's second Golden age of Hollywood, as the old studio system of the 1930's completely broke down and restrictions on sexual content, obscenity and violence loosened.
