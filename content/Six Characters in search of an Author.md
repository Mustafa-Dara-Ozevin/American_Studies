# Characters 

1. Father
2. Mother 
3. Step-Daughter
4. The Son
5. Madame Pace
6. Manager
7. Actors


# Play

## Act 1

==Scene Description:== 
- Appearance of impromptu play => Satire of real life.
- Playing a [[Luigi Pirandello]] play => Meta commentary 
- **The Book** metaphor for *Guidance in life/God* => Actors has a book to play by while the Six Characters do not

==Script:== 

> Is it my fault that France won't send us any more good comedies and we are reduced to putting on [[Luigi Pirandello]]'s works, where nobody plays understands anything, and the author plays fool with us all - Manager 


- Manager describes the essence of [[Theater of Absurd]]
- Six Characters are thrown into the scene as human is thrown into the world
- Father => Philosopher, mouth piece of [[Luigi Pirandello]]
- Mother => Wearing black is foreshadowing for death that did not happen yet

> Are you not accustomed to see characters created by an author spring to life in yourselves and face each other? Just because there is no "book" which contains us you refuse to believe... - The Father

Religious criticism 

- pg 676 Father monologue: With [[Modernism]] information and truth stops being a single, unified thing to decentralized, fragmented thing. 

## Act 2 

- Step-daughter start of act 2: Scene => world, people => actors
- Actors tries to act as the six characters but can never satisfy the characters. at last Manager gets frustrated and gets the characters to play itself. 
- The Father can't conceive being played by another person.
