# Paris Peace Conference  
  
## Summary  
The Paris Peace Conference was a meeting of the victorious Allied powers in 1919 to set the peace terms for the defeated Central Powers following World War I. The conference resulted in the Treaty of Versailles and the creation of the League of Nations.  
## Notes  
* The "Big Four" leaders—Woodrow Wilson (U.S.), David Lloyd George (Britain), Georges Clemenceau (France), and Vittorio Orlando (Italy)—dominated the proceedings.
* Wilson promoted his "14 Points," while European leaders focused more on punishing Germany and securing their own national interests.
* The resulting Treaty of Versailles imposed heavy reparations and territorial losses on Germany, which many historians believe contributed to the rise of WWII.
* The conference also redrew the map of Europe and the Middle East, creating several new nations.
