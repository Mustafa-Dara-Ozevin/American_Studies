Started by [[William Wordsworth]] and [[Samuel Taylor Coleridge]] in their manifesto Lyrical Ballads. Born as a reaction to [[18th century Neo-Classicism]]. Prioritized intuition over reason

- "Sickly pleasure", suffering is an important part of Romantic writing
- Romantics were predominatly anarchist, resisting the sturctures of society 
- Nature was source for an inspiration and gateway to escape from despair of the daily life
- [[Sublime]] was an important concept
