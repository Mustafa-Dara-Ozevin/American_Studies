Poems by [[William Wordsworth]]. It is alleged that it is written for his lover which was named Lucy however Wordsworth refused the answer the questions and settle the debate. 

# She Dwelt Among The Untrodden Ways

Advectives used to describe the woman:
- A maid whom there were none to praise 
- Fair as a star
- half hidden from the eye
- lived unknown 

persona yearns and mourns after the woman figure but also gets a **sickly pleasure** from the unspoiled nature of her. The lack of interaction and intimacy between persona and woman creates a tension that strengthens the emotion.


# I Travelled Among Unknown Men

Lovesickness is accompanied by Homesickness to England. Persona discovers his love for England when he travells oversea to European mainland. Once again the love is more intensified when the source of love is lost. Lucy takes a role of Muse that gives the poet to power to write.


# A Slumber Did My Spirit Seal

At the first stanza Lucy is alive and persona has no *humanly fears*. He felt like she would never age.
At the second stanza Lucy is dead but since she is now part of the earth daily course and one with the nature. They are still together
