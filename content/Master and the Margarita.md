A Novel written by [[Mikhail Bulgakov]] between 1928 and 1940 in the [[Soviet Union]]. A Censored version with several chapters edited out was first published in 1966 in Moscow Magazine. The manuscript was first published as a book in Paris

The story is about the Devil and his entourage visiting the officially atheist Soviet Union. During this visit Devil challenges the beliefs and criticizes the behavior of Soviet Citizens. 


### Chapter 1 

Starts with introduction of literary editor [[Mikhail Alexandrovich Berlioz]] and Poet [[Ivan Nikolayevich]]. The duo is sitting at [[Patriach's Ponds]] under summer heat when [[Mikhail Alexandrovich Berlioz]] suddenly is taken by a strange fear and sees a 7 feet tall, narrow shouldered half transparent man wearing a jockey cap and skimpy, checkered jacket.
