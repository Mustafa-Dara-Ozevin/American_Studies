# Modernism 

[[Modernism]] rejected the idea that life follows a perfect trajectory and improves 

##  Subversion of Time
Modernists argued that time isn't linear and experienced different by different people. Fitzgerald utilizes this idea with Benjamin's reverse time trajectory 


## Satire and Social COnformity 

- The tragedy isn't benjamin's age but his families and society refusal of accepting him as he is 
- HE is forced to play with kid toys despite being a 70 year old

##  Alienation and the fragmented self
- Benjamin doesn't see himself ass one unified person. he is described as having a "curious detachment" from his identity and describes his life as watching a life of a stranger
