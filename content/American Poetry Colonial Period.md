# American Poetry Colonial Period  
  
## Summary  
[[American_Poetry_MOC||American Poetry]] Started with Puritans. Puritan ideas continued to influence it for long after independence till [[Emerson]].

## Notes  
*Calvinistic principles*:
1. Total Depravity
2. Irresistible Grace
3. Limited Attraction
4. Predestination
5. Unconditional Election
*Features*:
- Hard working, strict, severe punishment for crimes
- religion is at very center
- Very well read (proficient with metaphysical poetry, Greek and Roman classics)
- Often produced religious poems (hymns and sermons)
- Patriarchal: God => Church => Husband
- Rhyming, stanzaic verses
- [[Ramusian Logic]] Look for the scripture in daily events
- Art is God's gift to be turned, finally, to God's service.
- Conceit, Acrostics, refined, eloquent language
