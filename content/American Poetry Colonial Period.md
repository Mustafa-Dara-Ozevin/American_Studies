# American Poetry Colonial Period  
  
## Summary  
[[American_Poetry_MOC||American Poetry]] Started with Puritans. Puritian ideas continiued to influence it for long after indepence till [[Emerson]].
## Notes  
*Calvinistic principles*:
1. Total Depravity
2. Irerristable Grace
3. Limited Attonment
4. Predestination
5. Unconditional Election
*Features*:
- Hard working, strict, severe punisment for crimes
- religion is at very at the center
- Very well read (proficient with metaphysical poetry, Greek and Roman classics)
- Often produced religious poems (hymms and sermons)
- Patriarchial: God => Church => Husband
- Rhtymic, stanzaic verses
- [[Ramusian Logic]] Look for the scripture in daily events
- Art is God's gift to be turned, finally, to God's service.
- Conceit, Acrostics, refined, eloquent language

*Authors*:
- [[Anna Bradstreet]]
- [[Edward Taylor]]
