# Dialouge  

## Summary  

Speech reveals personality
## Notes

*Speech reveals*: 
	- Values
	- background
	- education
	- social position
	- emotion
	- desires

- ==different characters should sound different==
- *voice* is *characterization*

### Forms of Dialouge 

| Summary                               | Indırect Dialouge                              | Direct Dialouge                                                           |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Conversation cundensed into narration | Conversation reported witohut direct quotation | Character's actual words                                                  |
| useful for moving quickly             | Gives the sense of exchange                    | Creates immediacy, Especially useful for conflict, discovery and decision |

*Tips for natural dialouge:*
1. Listen to others talking
2. REmember words choices different age groups use
3. read your dialagoue aloud
4. Avoid info dumping in the natural dialouge
5. Watch TV, radio, podcasts


### Dialouge must do More than One Thing

*dialouge can:*
- Charactize 
- give information
- establish setting
- create mood
- advance mood
- reveal conflict
- foreshadow
- reveal the past 
- develop theme



### Voice

*Character voice comse from:*
- Vcab.
- diciton
- syntax
- rhythm
- sentence length
- slang
- repetition


### Text

What the character says

### Subtext

what the character means, feels, wants or hides

*Characters may*:
- Avoid the truth
- conceal feeling
- manipulate
- protect themselves
- lie
- speak directly

Key Idea: The most important thing in the consercation may be what is ***NOT*** said

### "No" Dialouge

Conflict becones stronger when thet is disagreement
**NO** can mean:
- Disagreement
- refusal
- contracdiction
- evasion
- sarcasm
- changing the subject
- challenging the other person

*Formula:*
- Character A wants X
- Character B wants y
- Their desires collide.

### Pacing & Dialouge

*Fast*:
- Short exchanges
- interruptions
- short sentences
*Slow*:
* longer speeches 

### Dialouge rules

- Keep exposition when possible
- Avoid unneccesary monologues
- give cgharacters disitnctive voices
- "Said" as default
- don't use excessive adverbs
- Use action to comm. emotion
- Read dialouge aloud
- Make sure it is speakable
