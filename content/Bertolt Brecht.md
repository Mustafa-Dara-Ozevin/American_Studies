Political Theater

Gestalt Principles = Consciousness is founded on filling the gaps based on assumptions
6 principles:
- Simplicity
- Closure
- Continuance
- Similarity
- Proximity
- Alignment

Brecht used this principles in his **Verfremdungffekt** forcing the audience to focus.
Direct information pushed the audience into passive consumption. Brecht forces them to think by withholding the information.

Dramas include:
- didactic-agitprop
- historical dramas
- dramas of social critisim

2 important theories:
- Oragnum for theatre
- Messingkauf Dialougues


Was a devoted Marxist but unlike other leftist who thurned to realsim he found the social realistic form outdated. He believed it inspired inertia rather than action.
Art for art = puerlille Indulgence

Advocated "Epic Theater" instead a term borrowed from the director Edwin Piscator.

He interrupted the Aristotelian flow which takes the casual chain of events as granted and explored what could be done to change the course of events.

Isolate or frame specific onstage moments and subject them to analysis.

Nullified the empathy from the play by encouraging them to resist identifying with characters 

Einfühlüng(feeling into) vs Mitfühlüng(feeling with)

Deliberately destructive, launching from scene to scene; meant to replicate a circus
preferring to delineate the acting, set design, directing instead of unifying them. 

A distance between act and the actor that would allow actor to criticize 

characters => Alterable and being able to alter


Radical separation of elements:
struggle for supremacy between words, music and production
