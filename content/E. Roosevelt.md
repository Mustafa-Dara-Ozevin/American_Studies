# E. Roosevelt  
  
## Summary  
Eleanor Roosevelt was the longest-serving First Lady of the United States and a prominent advocate for civil rights, women's rights, and human rights. She redefined the role of the First Lady through her active involvement in politics and public life.  
## Notes  
* She served as a delegate to the United Nations and chaired the committee that drafted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
* Eleanor often acted as her husband's "eyes and ears," traveling extensively to report on the conditions of the American people.
* She was a vocal supporter of the African American community and resigned from the Daughters of the American Revolution in protest of their discrimination.
* She maintained a daily newspaper column, "My Day," where she shared her thoughts on social and political issues.
