The luxurious British ocean liner which was sunk by a german submarine. 1189 people including 128 american died as a result of the attack. It resulted in public opinion to turn against Germany and got America one step closer to joining the war. Secretary of State William Jennings Bryan approached the situation more cautiously, upon hearing the news about the Lusitania he told his wife "I wonder if the ship was carrying munitions of war... if she did carry them, this puts a different face on the whole matter! England has been using our citizens to protect he ammunition". It soon emerged that the Lusitania was indeed carrying munitions and Bryan urged for restrained US response. He urged [[Woodrow Wilson]] to be equally tough on his condemnation of both German and British violations.
