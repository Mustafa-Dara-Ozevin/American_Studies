# 18th century female voice  
  
## Summary  
The Age more of Reason that of Poetry. Under the influence of such philosophers as  [[John Locke]] and scientists like [[Isaac Newton]], the world become more reasonable and confortable because than [[American Poetry Colonial Period|Puritans]] thought to be.
## Notes  

- They gained confidence in their ability to understand the *God and his creation* through ***reason and observation***
- [[John Locke]]'s [[Tabula rasa]] man instead Calvisinist sinful man

### Women's Poetry

- [[Vindication of Rights of Women 1792]] as start of feminist movement
- They are coming form wealthy famillies, including [[Phillis Wheatley]] who had white and wealthy adaptive family
- They felt the patriarchial pressure of the society on them. The effects of this pressure was threefold:
	- A number of them accepted or pretended to accept the jaundiced male view of them
	- They often published over daring to publish their writings
	- Feminist tone, speakign against society's snide view of them
- Broadside poems => anonanymous, short, talking about daily life, subjective
