# New Deal 1  
  
## Summary  
The First New Deal (1933-1934) was the initial phase of Franklin D. Roosevelt's response to the Great Depression. It focused on the "Three Rs": Relief for the unemployed, Recovery of the economy, and Reform of the financial system.  
## Notes  
* Key programs included the Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC) and the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA).
* The Glass-Steagall Act was passed to separate commercial and investment banking and create the FDIC.
* The Agricultural Adjustment Act (AAA) aimed to raise crop prices by paying farmers to leave some land unplanted.
* This phase was characterized by the rapid passage of legislation during the "first hundred days."
