# Appearance  
  
### Appearance of Characterization

- Clothing
- Hairstyle
- Body
- Possessions
- Objects
- Smell
- Voice 
- Truth
- Touch
- Physical Movement

***Appearance != Reality***

*for example:*
- Looks confident => Feels insecure
- Looks innocent => Behaves aggresively
- Look succesfull => Fears faillure

*gap creates*:
- irony
- tension
- layer
- surprise
