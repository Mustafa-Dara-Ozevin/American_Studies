### From Kant's Critique of Judgement

The beautiful nature is connected with the form of object which has **definite borders**
The sublime on the other hand is found on shapeless object which is **boundless**
The satisfaction in the sublime does not involve positive pleasure(admiration, respect) but *Negative pleasure*

Sublime is a state of mind produced by a certain representation with which the reflective judgement is occupied. Not the object, the state of mind when confronting the object


### From Edmund Burke's ideas on sublime and Beautiful


Fitted in any sort oto excite the ideas of pain and danger is a source of **sublime**. 
When danger and pain is too close they don't give pleasure but at the certain distances with certain modifications, they may be delightful. 

The passion caused by the great and sublime in nature is **Astonishment**
Astonishment is the state of the soul which all its motions are suspended with some degree of horror
