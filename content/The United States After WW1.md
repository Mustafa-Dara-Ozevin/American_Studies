# Social and Economic changes after the war

## US joins the war
- Wilson and society wanted neutrality and impartially
- preparedness of war
- Sympathy for Allied power despite neutrality

Sinking of Lusitania changed the sentiment 
Russian revolution and allies being in dire need further aggravated US
Declaring of War on Germany 

Military contribution:
- Weapons 
- soldiers
- loans

Germany asking for peace

US rising to global power and broker(loans)
Expanded Federal government power
- rise of Nationalism

## US before WW1

### Social aspects:
- Woman fighting for Suffrage
- Massive European immigration: Economic growth but also overcrowding
- Segregation

### Economic aspects
- Rapid industrial growth
- US still owed money to European investors
- Poor working conditions, child labor

## US After WW1
### Social Changes
- Woman gained voting rights
- The Harlem Renaissance
- Immigration restricted by the immigration act of 1924

### Economic Changes:
- Mass production expanded
- US become global creditor nation
- Consumer goods boom
## Underlying Problems


### Economic problems
- Farmning crisis
- Unequal wealth 
- Stock Market Crash
### Social Problems
- Ongoing Racial inequaltiy 
- Problems with Laborers 
- Anti immigrant approaches
