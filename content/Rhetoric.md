# Rhetoric  
   
## Notes  
- Cogito Ergo Sum => I think, therefore I am
- Que Sera Sera => whatever will be, will be 
- De Facto => in reality, actually
> The fact that Chong was now the **de facto** director of their films had upset the balance of power.  
	 David Fear, _Rolling Stone_, 17 Mar. 2024

- de iure => By law
- Circullum Vitae[^1] => City
- Divide et impera => Divide and conquer
- Fiat Lux => Let there be light
- Veni vidi vici => I come, I see, I c conquered
- E Pluribus Unum => One out of many
- E tu Brute => And you Brutus
- Magnum Opus[^2]: Masterwork, greatest work
- Status quo ante bellum => situation before the war
- Statüko => Siyasette statüko yanlısı devleteler, ulusal veya uluslarası düzeni değistermek istemeyenleri ifade eder.
- Deus Ex Machina: God from the machine
- Homo Homi*ni* lupus: Man is the wolf *of men* - **Thomas Hobbes**
