
# Rhetoric

## Terra

1. *Territory* => L. "Terra" land, "orium" place
2. *Mediterranean* => L. "medius" middle, "terra" land
3. *Terraqueous* => L. "Terra" land, "Aqua" Water
4. *Terracotta* => L. "terra" Land. "Cocta" cooked, baked
5. *Terra incongito* => L. "terra" land, "incognito" unknown
	1. "in" without, prefix
	2. "cognitus" recognize


## Tıp - hastalıklar

1. *Insommina* => G. "In" Negative Prefix, "Sommnus" Sleep.
2. *claustrophobia* => G. "Claustrum" confined place, "phobos" fear
3. *Hypertension* => G. "Hyper" over, above, excess, L. "Tensio" stretching, straining
4. *Dermatitis* => G. "Derma" skin, G "itis" disease characterized with inflammation[^1] 
5. *Epilepsy* => G. "Epi" upon, above, G. "Lepsis" to take hold of, grasp or seize


## Tıp - Bölümler
1. *Cardiology* => L. "Cardio" heart, "Logia" the science of 
2. *Neurology* => "Neuron" nerve, "Logia" the science of 
3. *Psychiatry* => G. "Psyche" soul, "iatreia" treatment
4. *Pediatrics* => "Paidos" children, 
5. *Urology* => "urina" pee, "Logia" the science of
6. Dermatology => "Derma" skin, "Logia" the science of


## Tıp - Vücüt
1. *Pedicure* => L. "pedis" foot, L. "cura" care
2. *Dental* => L. "dens" tooth, L. "al" related to
3. *Abdominal* => L "Abdomen[^2]" belly, L. "al" related to
4. *Lingual* => L. "Lingua" Language/Tongue, L. "al" related to
5. *Facial* => L "facies" face, "al" related to



## Sayılar
1. *Octave* => L. "Octavus" Eighth, L. "Octava" Eighth day after the festival
2. *Monopoly* => "monos" single/alone, G. "polein" to sell
3. *Pentagram* => G. "Pente" Five, G. "Gramma" Line 
4. *Octopus* => G. "Okto" eight, G. "Pous" Foot
5. *Hectare* => L. "Hekaton" hundred, "area" area
6. *Monopoly* => "Monos" only one, G. "Arkhe" power, rule


## Hukuk

1. *Perjury*[^3] => L. "per" away, harmfully or wrongly, L. "Jurare" to swear
2. *Allegation* => L. "allegare" bring forward a plea or excuse
	1. L. "ad" to/toward
	2. L. "Legare" to send on a commission, "to appoint,"or "to charge
3. *Subpoena*[^4] => L. "Sub" under/beneath, "Poena" punishment, penalty or fine
4. *Malfeasance* => L. "Mal" evil/bad, "Facere" to do/make
5. *De Facto* => L. "de" From, Of or Concerning. "Facto" deed, act or event.
    
---
[^1]: Enfeksiyon
[^2]: Perhaps [OED, Watkins] from *abdere* "conceal" (from ab "off, away" + PIE root dhe- "to set, put"), with a sense of "concealment of the viscera," or else "what is concealed" by proper dress.
[^3]: it means "swearing wrongly" or "breaking an oath."
[^4]: Adopted from the phrasing used in the opening lines of the original English writs, which ordered a person to appear in court sub poena—literally translating to "under penalty" of a specific fine or imprisonment if they failed to show up.
