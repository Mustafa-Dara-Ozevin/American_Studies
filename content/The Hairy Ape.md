Is an example of [[Expressionism]]


Written in 1921 bu Eugene O'Neill



* Is about a laborer, Yank who is portrayed as an unthinking , ape like person.
* The Change of Yank and his search for his identity and sense of belonging are the most important themes of the play

- Long (Communist - Class Consciousness)
- Mildred Douglas
- Her aunt
- Second Engineer
- A Guard
- A secretary of an Organization 
- Stoker, Ladies, Gentleman etc




Hairy Ape was a symbol of a man, who has lost his old harmony with nature, the harmony which he used to have as an animal and has not yet acquired in a spiritual way

### Opening Description
in a transatlantic liner, in the engine room
